Nothing Desktop

I love the aestetic of Nothing products. They're clean, and get straight to the point, no distraction, no time lost here.
Nothing make phone and earbuds, but no computer and a lot of users seem to be asking for one, and I have aready seens some comcept art online and I thought "Why not trying to remake it with linux", I choose archlinux for the good Hyprland Support (A window manager, graphical desktop if you prefer) and quickshell to code my own bar and widgets the Nothing way. I am thinking about publishing it as a standalone ISO that could be installed and use as the "Nothing Desktop" (this is not a real "new" distro in a way, this is just arch with personnal dotfiles preinstalled and a graphical installer, a bit like omarchy in a way) {note for me: put the screenshots here later } Dotfiles
